---
name: Scenario forecast calibration
description: Guidance for path-comparison visuals and prediction claims.
---

Until ALTER EGO has observed outcomes or validated forecast data, scenario charts must use qualitative relative labels and must not imply numerical probabilities or measured predictions. Keep assumptions and limitations visible.

**Why:** The current comparison is derived from the user's wording and locally allowed context; there is no outcome dataset or calibrated model to support percentage claims. Unsupported probability claims could mislead on consequential personal decisions.

**How to apply:** When changing scenario cards, recommendations, confidence labels, or charts, use qualitative levels unless a measured data source and calibration are established and clearly labeled.
